Saturday 20 March

The day

Everything happens in one place, so once you're here you can put the car keys away.

  1. 4:30 pm

    Arrive

    Park in the main carpark and make your way down to the lawn. Please be in the ceremony area by 5:00pm. Grab a parasol on the way; March in the valley can turn on the heat.

  2. 5:00 pm

    Ceremony

    On the lawn, between the lake and the restaurant. Seating is unassigned. We only ask that you leave the first rows for immediate family.

  3. 5:30 pm

    Drinks & photos

    Canapés and something cold in the courtyard while we disappear briefly for photographs.

  4. 6:00 pm

    Dinner

    Scan the QR code at the door to find your table. Name cards are set at each place.

  5. 11:00 pm

    Farewell

    A bus will be running at the end of the night to take you home. Please let us know in your RSVP if you would like a seat.

Parking

Free parking on site, and you're welcome to leave the car overnight and collect it the next morning.

Shuttle

Ubers and taxis are slim pickings in the Yarra Valley, so we're organising a shuttle home. It leaves shortly after the evening concludes and will drop you at your address. Let us know in your RSVP if you'd like a seat.

Where to sleep

Healesville is ten minutes up the highway and has the most beds. Yarra Glen and Coldstream are closer but book out early, so we'd sort it by December.

Formal

What to wear

Formal wedding attire. Suits and ties, cocktail or floor-length dresses. There's no colour scheme to follow and nothing to co-ordinate with.

Worth knowing

Late March in the valley is warm through the afternoon and properly cool once the sun drops behind the range, so bring a layer you're happy to be photographed in.

The ceremony is on grass. Please keep that in mind when choosing footwear.

The colours of the day, for the curious. Not something we expect anyone to match.
